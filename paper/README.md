# Paper workspace

Working title: **Which training data makes deep hedging work on real Hong Kong stocks?**

- `outline.md` — structure, research question and pre-registered hypotheses (filled in as the study runs)
- `references.bib` — references, each verified against its publisher or arXiv page

Numbers in the paper are taken only from `results/` and charts only from `figures/`, both regenerated by `scripts/run_all.sh`.
